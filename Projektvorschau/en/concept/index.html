Concept scope · Basis for decision

From website to system.

The public website, member area and editorial system work together. Scope is based on the publicly documented priorities of the DENOG board and working groups.

Four guardrails

What the solution must deliver.

Not every module has to be fully developed on day one. What matters is a sustainable architecture that accelerates the public relaunch and can gradually integrate internal processes.

01

Content first

Clear structure, a strong elevator pitch, current information and consistently maintained German/English versions.

02

Working Groups

Scope, chairs, channels, publications, GitHub and events become transparent for every group.

03

Membership

A simple application plus self-service for profile, status, documents, participation and identity.

04

Digital independence

Open software, portable data, documented interfaces and hosting without avoidable platform lock-in.

Technical framework

Open, accessible, maintainable.

Open-source CMS with a custom theme, mobile-first, WCAG 2.1 AA, privacy-compliant integrations, performant delivery, roles and permissions, and editorial preview.

12 weeks to go-live.
